Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

AutoPost AI Studio is an automated personal content creation tool designed to assist users in generating and uploading short-form video content to TikTok.

2. Data We Collect

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. When you use AutoPost Studio with TikTok's API, the application accesses:

  • Your TikTok OAuth access token to publish videos authorized by you.
  • Basic public profile identifiers necessary for authentication.
  • Video files and metadata (titles, captions, hashtags) created by your prompts.

3. How We Use Data

All authentication credentials (such as API keys and OAuth tokens) remain securely stored in your local environment file (.env) and are utilized solely to interact with TikTok APIs for video uploads initiated by you.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

User tokens and generated media can be deleted at any time by clearing your local output directory or updating your configuration.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact the developer through your project repository.
